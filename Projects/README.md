## Project principles

I want my projects to demonstrate more than a finished UI. For each substantial project, I aim to document:

- The problem I'm solving
- Why I chose the approach
- Architecture and design decisions
- What I learned
- Trade-offs and limitations
- What I would build next

More projects will be added as they become real enough to be worth sharing.
