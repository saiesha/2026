Explore Collatz sequences continuously. Generate more starting numbers whenever you want.

Continuous exploration
Starts at 1 and extends the range by 1 crore (10,000,000) numbers per click.

Rule: if n is even, the next number is n ÷ 2. If n is odd, the next number is 3n + 1. The step count is the number of transitions needed to reach 1.

Generating Collatz chains…
Only maxVerified is retained as Collatz result state. Every number from 1 through maxVerified has been verified to reach 1. Visible chains are recalculated when you view a page; they are not stored. A detected cycle or unsafe integer stops verification.
