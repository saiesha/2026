Collatz Linked Lists

Every number up to your limit, plus every extra number needed to complete its path to 1. Shared values appear once.

For each number n from 1 to the limit, follow n → n/2 (even) or n → 3n+1 (odd) until reaching 1. Any values above the limit encountered along the way are included too. Each node has one parent (its next Collatz value), and at most two children.
